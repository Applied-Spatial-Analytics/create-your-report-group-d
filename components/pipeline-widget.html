Pipeline overview

1
Extract data — Raster (LCZ, LST, NDVI) and vector data (administrative units, roads) are extracted.
2
Preproces raster data — Land Surface Temperature is normalised to enable consistent comparison across neighbourhoods and time periods.
3
Process vector data — At the neighbourhood level, mean LST, mean NDVI, and the dominant LCZ class were computed. Network betweenness, LST, NDVI, and LCZ were calculated for each road segment.
4
Identify hot neighbourhoods — Neighbourhoods showing distinctly high LST are identified as thermal hotspots for further analysis.
5
Street-segment clustering — Road segments are grouped into clusters using K-Means based on NDVI, LST, and betweenness.
6
Describe clusters — Clusters are characterised through Google Street View imagery (SVI) to verify thermal and land cover conditions on the ground.
7
Propose nature-based solutions — Nature-based solutions for cooling are proposed based on the SVI.
8
Compare the cities — Carry out comparison of LCZ, PST, and mean NDVI between the two cities.
